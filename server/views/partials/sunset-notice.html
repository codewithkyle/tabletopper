Tabletopper is moving

This version of Tabletopper will shut down on April 3, 2027. The new Tabletopper is already live at beta.tabletopper.app and will replace it.

New for game masters and players:

  • Full character sheets with spells and spell slots, attacks, inventory, a coin purse and class resources
  • Player journals with images, and share links for characters, journals and monsters
  • An asset library for your maps, tokens, avatars, terrain and music
  • Map layers, plus scenes that save a whole tabletop to reopen later
  • Hex grids with terrain tiles, and drawing tools for lines, rectangles, circles and cones
  • Your own music, played in sync for the whole table

Rather try something else? Owlbear Rodeo is a simple browser-based alternative.
